WebGL texImage2D SIGBUS repro — crashing case

This crashes the driver (Mesa zink/nvk on NVIDIA Turing via nouveau) regardless of setup. On Firefox under native Wayland specifically, that crash can freeze the whole system -- save your work first if that's your setup. Under Chromium, or Firefox forced into XWayland, it stays a contained crash instead.

Uploads a single 8192×8192 RGBA8 texture (268,435,456 bytes) via texImage2D. This is the same size/format as the crash originally found on a real site (a large single-texture upload), and matches a minimal headless (non-browser) EGL reproduction of the same fault. See the contrasting non-crashing pages in this directory: a smaller texture (no_crash_small_texture.html) and the same size under induced VRAM pressure (no_crash_vram_pressure.html).
